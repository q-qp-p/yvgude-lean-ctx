# Historical / Research — hosted-index operations

> **Unshipped service concept.** LeanCTX does not currently offer a hosted
> index, public service-level objective, hosted storage plan, or paid hosted
> index product.

This file previously described operational targets for a future managed
service. Those targets are not a customer commitment, pricing promise, or
runbook for an available LeanCTX deployment.

## Current boundary

The Available product is local-first: local Runtime, CLI, MCP, proxy,
context-selection/compression/reuse primitives, and local evidence foundations.
Cloud, managed indexes, account services, control planes, organization
dashboards, and enterprise operations are **Research** until an explicit
commercial product decision and the required proof, security, support, and
rollback gates exist.

Do not quote or operationalize any historical latency, availability, freshness,
pricing, plan, or GA target from earlier versions of this document.
