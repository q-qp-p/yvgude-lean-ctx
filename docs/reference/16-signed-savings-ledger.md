# Local receipt primitives and evidence limits

**Status: Available local substrate.** LeanCTX contains local receipt,
evidence, and offline-verification primitives. They make an artifact
inspectable; they do not turn a local counter into an unqualified savings,
quality, financial, compliance, or business-outcome claim.

## What integrity evidence establishes

Where a specific local artifact supports it, a signature or verification result
can establish that the artifact has not changed since signing and identifies
the key that signed it. It does not independently establish that the underlying
workload was comparable, the quality was preserved, a cost basis was correct,
or the result applies outside the declared run.

## Required claim discipline

A performance statement requires all of the following:

1. A comparable baseline and treatment.
2. A declared quality threshold.
3. The cost basis and methodology used for the comparison.
4. Inspectable evidence that states its visibility limits.

A cheaper failed task is not a win. Do not use a signed local artifact as a
billing record, finance assertion, public benchmark result, or universal
percentage claim without that full evidence chain.

## Product boundary

The local Receipt and offline-verification foundations are available. Common
session/Receipt convergence remains **Preview**; benchmark, quality-comparison,
and canonical evidence-bundle work remains **Research**. Organization audit
services, shared evidence history, and hosted operations are **Research**, not
a public LeanCTX offering.
