# Historical / Research — Team, Cloud & CI

> **No hosted, team, cloud, account, or enterprise service is publicly
> available from this repository.** This file preserves the boundary around
> earlier implementation and service concepts; it is not an operations guide or
> a sales promise.

## Available local path

LeanCTX is local-first. The supported Runtime consists of local CLI, MCP, proxy,
and supported coding-agent setup paths, together with local context
selection/compression/reuse and local receipt or offline-verification
primitives. A local workflow remains useful without an account or a hosted
control plane.

CI and automation may use the local Runtime where its individual command and
security contracts permit it. An integration must state what it can observe;
it cannot claim host-only prompts, retries, decisions, or outcome quality that
it did not observe.

## Historical / research material

Earlier documents and implementation directories mention shared indexes,
accounts, sync, connectors, hosted dashboards, billing, organization controls,
and managed services. They are not a LeanCTX public product or dependency.
Their presence is not evidence of availability, support, service levels,
retention, security certification, or pricing.

Organization-scale operation, private deployment, and shared evidence are
private-commercial **Research** directions. They cannot enter the OSS critical
path ahead of an explicit product decision and the required proof, ownership,
and rollback gates.

## Evidence boundary

Local observations and signed artifacts may help inspect one declared run.
They do not establish a universal savings percentage, an invoice-ready cost
claim, service-level commitment, or a business outcome. Any performance claim
requires a comparable baseline and treatment, declared quality threshold,
methodology, and verifiable evidence.
